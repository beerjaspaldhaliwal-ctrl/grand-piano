GRAND PIANO - installing in FL Studio (Windows)
===============================================

1. Copy the folder "Grand Piano.vst3" into:

       C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\

2. Open FL Studio and go to  Options > Manage plugins  and click
   "Find installed plugins" (or "Find more plugins").
   "Grand Piano" appears under Generators. Tick the star to add it to your favourites.

3. Add it to the Channel Rack: click "+" in the Channel Rack (or Add > More plugins)
   and choose "Grand Piano".

4. Piano samples
   - If you downloaded the "WithSamples" package, the samples are inside the plugin
     already - nothing else to do.
   - Otherwise click the gold "Get Samples" button in the plugin. It downloads the free
     Salamander Grand Piano (Yamaha C5 concert grand, 16 velocity layers, ~740 MB) into
       %LOCALAPPDATA%\Grand Piano\
     Until it finishes the plugin plays a built-in modelled piano.

   The first time the samples load, the plugin prepares them for disk streaming
   (10-60 seconds, about 1.3 GB in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Grand Piano\Cache). After that it
   loads in about a second.

Tips
 - Use the preset menu (top centre) - "Studio Pop" and "Bright Rock" give the
   produced, upfront sound of commercial pop pianos; "Concert Grand" is natural.
 - Hammer = brighter/mellower tone without changing loudness.
 - Touch = velocity response (turn right if your keyboard feels too heavy).
 - Stretch = 0% exact equal temperament, 100% the piano's own recorded stretch tuning.
 - "Grand Piano.exe" is a standalone version for playing without FL Studio.
